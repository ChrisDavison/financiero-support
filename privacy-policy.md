# Financiero Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Financiero is an iOS app for recording income, expenses, budgets, savings goals and recurring bills. It is developed and operated by **Chris Davison, United Kingdom**.

This policy explains how information is handled in the app and when you use its public GitHub support repository. For privacy questions or requests, use [Financiero support issues](https://github.com/ChrisDavison/financiero-support/issues). **Issues are public. Do not include financial records, exports, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.** You can begin a privacy request with a general description and a link to the relevant issue or comment, without posting additional personal details.

## Information you enter in the app

Financiero stores the information you choose to record, including:

- Transactions: amounts, currencies, exchange rates, dates, payees, notes, categories and ownership labels.
- Categories and budgets, savings goals and contributions, including their names, amounts, dates and notes.
- Recurring bills and subscriptions, their schedules, and reminder titles and settings.
- App preferences, such as your default ownership label, exchange-rate setting and app-lock preference.
- Record identifiers and timestamps used to organise this information.

The app uses these records to show your finances, calculate summaries, manage goals and budgets, schedule reminders and generate exports. It does not connect to your bank or request bank login credentials. You do not create a separate Financiero account.

## Device storage and iCloud

Your financial records are stored on your device. Financiero also uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync records through a **private database in your iCloud account** when iCloud is available. The app attempts to set up iCloud storage automatically; there is no separate sync switch inside Financiero. If that storage setup fails, the app uses local storage instead. Successful syncing depends on your iCloud account, settings and service availability.

Your private iCloud financial records are not available to Chris Davison through a developer dashboard, and Financiero does not send them to a developer-operated server. Apple operates iCloud and handles information under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage iCloud access and storage through Apple's device and account settings.

Device backups and iCloud backups may also contain app data, depending on your settings. Those backups are separate from the app's live records.

## Notifications and app locking

With your permission, Financiero schedules local notifications on your device. A reminder may display its title; a bill notification may display the payee, amount and due information. Notification previews may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your iOS settings. You can change or disable reminders in the app and revoke notification permission or hide previews in iOS Settings.

If you enable the app lock, iOS handles authentication using Face ID or your device passcode. Financiero receives the authentication result, not your biometric data or passcode. You can disable the app lock in Financiero's settings.

## Exports and sharing

You can export records as CSV files or a JSON backup through the app's export screen. Financiero creates export files in temporary storage on your device and opens Apple's share interface so you can choose a destination. An export may contain financial and personal information from your records.

Exports are not automatically sent to Chris Davison. If you share or save an export, the recipient or storage service may handle it under its own policies. You control those copies and must remove them separately when you no longer need them. Temporary export files may remain until they are replaced, removed with the app, or cleaned up by iOS.

## Analytics, advertising and diagnostics

Financiero includes no advertising, tracking, analytics or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not sell your personal information or use your financial records for marketing. It has no bank connection or external financial-data service.

Apple may process App Store, device or diagnostic information under its own policies and your Apple settings. This is separate from a tracking or analytics service built into Financiero.

## Public GitHub support

Technical support is provided through [public GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ChrisDavison/financiero-support/issues). If you open an issue or comment, Chris Davison can see your GitHub username, public profile information and anything you submit, including attachments, screenshots and technical details. Other people can see the same public content.

This information is used to respond to requests, investigate faults and maintain the app. The UK data-protection basis for this use is **legitimate interests**: providing technical support and fixing problems, while limiting requests to information needed for those purposes. Providing information through support is optional and is not required to use the app.

Issues and comments remain public unless their content is removed; closing an issue does not delete it. They may remain available as a technical-support history without a fixed deletion date. You can edit or delete your own comments where GitHub permits, or request removal of personal information through the support repository. Removal from the repository cannot guarantee removal of copies already made by others, search engines or archives. GitHub controls its own logs, backups and retention.

GitHub also handles account and service information under its [General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Its infrastructure may process information outside the UK, including in the United States; its statement explains its international-transfer safeguards. Apple and GitHub operate their own services and handle privacy requests about those services directly.

## Keeping and deleting your app data

Financiero has no automatic expiry period for financial records: they remain until you remove them or the relevant storage is deleted. You can edit records and delete transactions, goals, contributions, recurring items and reminders using the controls available in the app. Some categories can be archived rather than deleted when they are in use.

You can also choose **More → Delete all data** and confirm deletion to remove financial records, goals, recurring items and reminders, and reset categories and budgets to their defaults. This does not reset every app preference or remove exported files.

When iCloud sync is working, record changes and deletions are intended to sync to your other devices. Syncing may take time and requires service availability.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage, but does not necessarily remove its iCloud data, backups or exports. Manage any remaining iCloud data and backups through Apple's settings, and delete exported copies from wherever you saved or shared them. Turning off iCloud access does not by itself erase existing cloud records. Chris Davison cannot retrieve or erase your private iCloud financial records on your behalf.

## Your privacy rights

For personal information Chris Davison handles through support, applicable UK data-protection law gives you rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

**You have the right to object to the use of your support information based on legitimate interests.** Raise privacy requests through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ChrisDavison/financiero-support/issues), without posting sensitive information. If a request needs identity verification or confidential details, do not post those details publicly; a suitable way to handle them must be agreed before they are supplied.

For records stored in the app, use the app's editing, deletion and export controls and your Apple storage settings. For information GitHub or Apple handles independently, contact the relevant service using its privacy-policy contact options.

You may also complain to the UK's [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes to this policy

The current policy is published in the Financiero support repository. If data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Any new use requiring permission will require that permission before it begins.
